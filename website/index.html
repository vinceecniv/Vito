Bring Your Own Key

New to "bring your own key"? It's simpler than it sounds.

Instead of paying Vito, you make your own free account with the AI providers, generate a personal API key — a secret password that lets software use the service for you — and paste it into Vito once. From then on Vito talks to those services directly, using your account. It's your key, your data, your bill. There is no Vito server in the middle.

Questions

Good to know

Is Vito really free?

Yes. The app is free and open source. You only pay the AI providers directly for what you dictate — typically a few euros a month, and nothing at all in months you don't use it.

What does "bring your own key" mean for me?

You create free accounts with the providers, generate an API key, and paste it into Vito once. Vito then uses your account directly. Nothing is routed through a Vito server, and you're billed pay-as-you-go by the providers.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows and Linux today, with macOS coming soon. It's one small binary with a web-based interface — no heavy app framework.

Where does my voice data go?

Only to the speech-to-text provider (for the audio) and, if cleanup is on, to Anthropic (for the transcript). Nothing else leaves your machine, and there's no tracking of any kind.

Why the name "Vito"?

It stands for Voice In, Text Out — which is exactly what it does.

Can I read or change the source?

Absolutely. Vito is open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence — read it, audit it, fork it. Code contributions aren't accepted upstream, but forking is welcome and guaranteed by the licence.
