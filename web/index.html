AI cleanup

Good to know: in the browser Vito uses Whistle, a small speech model — fine for trying, not the best recognition. Better: Soniox with your own key, or the Vito app.

Vito listens to your voice and pastes the finished text right where your cursor is. Here's how to get started:

  1. Choose how your speech becomes text, under Settings → Speech recognition:
    • Cloud (Soniox or AssemblyAI): the most accurate, live transcript while you speak, all languages — needs a free API key; from $0.10 per hour of audio after the starting credit.
    • Vito local: free, works offline, nothing leaves your computer — one download of about 1 GB; 25 European languages, the text arrives after you stop. Best for privacy.
  2. Want automatic AI cleanup? Add a key for Anthropic (Claude) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — Groq has a free tier, or point Vito at a local model.
  3. Set your start/stop hotkey under Settings → Activation and review the other settings.
  4. Press your hotkey, speak, and Vito pastes the text where your cursor is.

Tip: each API-key field on the settings page has a 'Get a key' button with step-by-step instructions to request that key.

Cleanup rules

The instructions Vito gives the model. Vito's own sets are always here and can't be changed — pick the one that fits what you dictate, or start your own from it.

A Vito Assist command (“Vito, …”) doesn't use these rules: carrying out an instruction replaces them rather than extending them. The dictionary corrections and the language are sent separately, so you don't need to repeat them here.

Vito Assist

Say “Vito, …” and a short instruction. It isn't typed out — it's applied to your next dictation, or to the text already on your clipboard.

How it works
  1. Say “Vito, …” with your instruction (e.g. “Vito, translate to English”), then stop as usual — with the hotkey, or by pausing so it auto-stops.
  2. You hear the Vito Assist tone, and the microphone reopens on its own.
  3. Now speak your text.
  4. Stop with the hotkey as usual, or just wait for the automatic submit.

The instruction is applied to that dictation, so what gets pasted is the result — not the command. A clipboard command needs no second step: it runs right away.

Examples
Reshape your dictation
“Vito, translate to English”
“Vito, summarise this”

Ask it something

Work on your clipboard
“Vito, summarise the text on the clipboard”
“Vito, translate the clipboard to Dutch”
The result goes back to your clipboard, ready to paste.
What it can't do

Vito Assist is text in, text out. It can't generate images, search the internet, or perform actions (open apps, send mail) — so “Vito, draw a dancing horse” won't work.

Vito Assist only works when AI cleanup is on — that pass is what carries out the command.
Model

Give Vito Assist its own, heavier model — answering and transforming ask more of a model than tidy-up does.

Backup & restore

A backup holds all your Vito data — settings, dictionary, history, statistics and achievements — in one file. Audio recordings are not included.

Backups are lightly encrypted with a built-in key, so they aren't plainly readable on disk. Because every Vito shares that key it's a deterrent against casual reading — not full security — so still treat a backup as sensitive.

⚠ A backup contains your API keys. Keep it somewhere safe and don't share it.
Manual backup
Automatic backups

Vito keeps the 4 most recent automatic backups in its data folder, so you always have a way back if something goes wrong.

Sync between computers

Keep your dictations, statistics and costs, achievements, dictionary and cleanup rules the same on every computer, through a folder your own sync app keeps in sync — Dropbox, OneDrive, Nextcloud, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Syncthing or a network share. Vito talks to no server for this.

Deleting a dictation or giving it a star carries over too. Settings and API keys stay on each computer: microphones, hotkeys and providers often differ between them.

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) (Bring your own key)

Vito uses a "Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) model: you use your own API keys (AssemblyAI or Soniox for speech recognition, Anthropic or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint for the AI cleanup). Vito charges you nothing itself and sends your audio or text nowhere except to the providers your keys belong to.

Providing, managing and securing those keys — and any costs their use incurs at the providers — are entirely your own responsibility.

Privacy & data

Vito does not collect any user data to send anywhere, and there is no tracking or analytics of any kind built into the app. Your settings, dictionary and history stay local on your own computer.

The only data the app sends externally is your speech and the texts that go to the external BYOK providers (speech recognition and AI cleanup). That data is governed by those external providers’ privacy policies.
